## Presentation Tips

### For Delivery:
1. **Start Strong:** Lead with the problem (Slide 1) - everyone relates to chaotic group planning
2. **Show, Don't Tell:** Use screenshots and demos extensively
3. **Tell Stories:** Use real user scenarios throughout
4. **Build Momentum:** Progress from problem → solution → details → vision
5. **End with Action:** Clear call-to-action on final slide

### For Design:
1. **Consistent Branding:** Use TripTogether colors and fonts throughout
2. **High-Quality Images:** Travel photography for emotional connection
3. **Minimal Text:** Let visuals do the talking
4. **Animations:** Subtle transitions, avoid distractions
5. **White Space:** Don't overcrowd slides

### For Audience:
- **Investors:** Focus on slides 33-40 (business model, market, roadmap)
- **Users:** Focus on slides 8-14 (how it works, features)
- **Partners:** Focus on slides 15-20, 34 (features, competitive advantages)
- **Technical:** Focus on slides 26-32, 36 (architecture, IA)
